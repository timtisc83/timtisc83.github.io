Privacy Policy

ERP Loop Tape · Last updated: August 2, 2026

Summary

ERP Loop Tape does not collect, transmit, or store any personal data on external servers. Everything stays on your device.

Data Storage

All data you create in the app (audio recordings, words, settings) is stored exclusively in your device's local browser storage (IndexedDB and localStorage). No data is sent to any server, cloud service, or third party.

Data Collection

We do not collect:

  • Personal information (name, email, location)
  • Usage analytics or telemetry
  • Crash reports
  • Advertising identifiers
  • Any other data

Third-Party Services

The app does not integrate any third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking services.

Microphone Access

The app requests microphone permission solely to record audio scripts that you choose to create. Recordings are stored locally on your device and are never transmitted.

Children's Privacy

The app is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect data from anyone, including children.

Medical Disclaimer

ERP Loop Tape is not a medical device. It does not provide therapy, diagnosis, or treatment recommendations. It is a simple audio playback tool intended to support exposure exercises under the guidance of a qualified mental health professional.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted at this URL with a new date.
